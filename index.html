Final Project

This site showcases some of the most iconic members of DC Comics' Justice League, the team of superheroes first assembled to face threats too large for any one hero to handle alone. Each figure below highlights a founding or long-standing member of the team, paired with the name most fans know them by.

The Justice League brings together heroes with wildly different origins and powers — from Superman's near-invulnerability and Wonder Woman's Amazonian strength, to Batman's detective skill and Green Lantern's willpower-fueled energy constructs. Together with allies like The Flash, Aquaman, Hawkgirl, and Martian Manhunter, they defend Earth from threats ranging from street-level crime to cosmic-scale invasions led by villains such as Darkseid.
